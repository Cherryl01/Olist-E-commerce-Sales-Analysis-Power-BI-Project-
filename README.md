## About the Project

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed using the Olist Brazilian E-commerce dataset. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that help evaluate sales performance, product category contribution, and revenue trends over time.

The project demonstrates the complete Business Intelligence workflow, including data preparation, data modeling, DAX measure creation, interactive dashboard development, and business insight generation.

---

# Business Questions Answered

1. What is the total revenue generated by the business?

2. How has revenue changed over the years?

3. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?

4. Which product categories contribute the least to total sales?

5. What sales trends can be observed over time?

6. Which business areas present opportunities for revenue growth?

# Dashboard & Key Findings

### 1. Revenue Trend by Year

> **Insight:** Revenue has changed across different years, allowing stakeholders to identify business growth patterns and evaluate overall sales performance over time.

---

### 2. Top Product Categories by Revenue

> **Insight:** A small number of product categories contribute a significant proportion of total revenue, highlighting the company's highest-performing product segments.

---

### 3. Total Revenue KPI

> **Insight:** The Total Revenue KPI provides an overall measure of business performance and serves as the primary indicator for monitoring sales growth.

---

### 4. Product Category Performance

> **Insight:** Product categories vary considerably in revenue contribution, helping identify both high-performing categories and those requiring strategic improvement.

---

### 5. Interactive Dashboard Analysis

> **Insight:** Interactive filters and visualizations enable users to explore sales performance dynamically, making it easier to identify trends and support data-driven decision-making.

## Getting Started

To explore this dashboard on your local machine:

### Prerequisites

Ensure you have the following installed:

* Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* Olist Brazilian E-commerce Dataset (if refreshing the data)

---

### Installation

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Cherryl01/Olist-Ecommerce-PowerBI-Analysis
```

Place the dataset files in the project directory if you intend to refresh the data.

Example:

```
Olist-Ecommerce-PowerBI-Analysis/
│
├── Olist Analysis.pbix
├── README.md
├── Dataset/
└── Images/
```

Open the project:

```text
Open "Olist Analysis.pbix" using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
```

Refresh the dataset if necessary to recreate the dashboard.

# Executive Recommendations

Based on the dashboard insights, the following recommendations are proposed:

### Focus on High-Revenue Product Categories

Increase inventory availability and marketing investment for top-performing product categories to maximize revenue growth.

### Improve Low-Performing Categories

Investigate underperforming categories by evaluating pricing strategies, customer demand, and promotional campaigns.

### Monitor Revenue Trends Continuously

Use yearly revenue trends to forecast future performance and support strategic planning.

### Expand Data-Driven Decision Making

Leverage interactive dashboards to monitor business KPIs regularly and enable faster, evidence-based decisions across departments.

### Enhance Future Dashboards

Future improvements could include customer segmentation, regional sales analysis, profitability analysis, delivery performance, and predictive sales forecasting to provide deeper business insights.
